The journey

From design
to completion

This is how your NMK home comes to life: clear, personal and step by step.

01

Orientation & advice

You explore the models online, view them in 3D and place them on your own plot. Then we discuss your wishes, your location and how you'll use the home in a no-obligation consultation.

02

Configuration & quote

In the configurator you choose your model, materials, kitchen, bathroom and extra options. Within 2 working days you'll receive a complete quote with specifications, prices and planning.

03

Location & permit

Whether you need a permit depends on your location, the intended use and your municipality's regulations. Together we look at your plot and what is required.

04

Production

Your home is built modularly and finished according to your configuration: from facade and frames to kitchen, bathroom and installations.

05

Transport & foundation

The home is transported to your location and placed on a foundation suited to your plot and ground conditions.

06

Placement & handover

We place and connect the home, inspect everything and deliver it turnkey, ready to use.

Good to know

Transport, foundation, placement and permit

Transport

The home is transported by road to your location. The accessibility of your plot determines which vehicles, and possibly which crane, are needed. We include transport for your location in your quote.

Foundation

A modular home stands on a foundation tailored to your ground conditions and your plot. We discuss which solution fits based on your location. The foundation and utility connections depend on your situation and are listed separately in your quote.

Placement & connection

After placement, the home is connected to electricity, water and sewerage. Inside the home, everything is already fully installed.

Permits

Regulations differ by municipality and by use, for example recreational use, permanent residence or informal care. We think along with you and help you with the information you need for your application.

You always receive a schedule and the costs for transport, foundation and placement in your personal quote.
