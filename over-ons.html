About NMK Living

Modular homes for a better tomorrow.

NMK Living designs and builds luxury modular homes that can be placed anywhere. With premium materials, large glazed walls and full turnkey delivery, we make living in nature accessible, comfortable and sustainable.

Sustainable & energy-efficient

Fully insulated and ready for solar energy and a heat pump.

Premium finish

Wood, metal and natural stone, finished down to the last detail.

Delivered turnkey

From permit to placement: we take care of the entire process.

Whether you're a private individual or a business: we build for everyone. A home of your own, a holiday retreat, a rental unit on a holiday park, a garden office or a granny annexe for informal care. You configure every model yourself, and we deliver it turnkey.
