Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Can't find your question? Email us at info@nmkliving.com or book a consultation.

What does a modular home from NMK Living cost?

The Aurora 24 starts from € 39.900, the Aurora 36 from € 49.900 and the Aurora 42 from € 59.900, excluding VAT. For the Aurora Villa we create a custom price. Your chosen options, transport and placement are in your personal quote.

What is included as standard?

Materials and complete installation, a kitchen with appliances, bathroom and sanitary ware, air conditioning and ventilation, electricity, water and sewerage in the home, LED lighting inside and out, and a complete interior and exterior finish.

Do I need a permit?

That depends on your location, the intended use and your municipality's regulations. We're happy to think along with you and look together at what your plot requires.

How large are the homes?

All models are 4,00 m wide with a ceiling height of 2,50 m. The length grows with the number of m²: from 6,00 m (24 m²) to 18,00 m (72 m²).

Can I configure the home myself?

Yes. In the configurator you choose the facade, floor, walls, ceiling, kitchen, bathroom, installations and extras, and view the result in 3D, inside and out.

How do transport, foundation and placement work?

The home is transported in sections or in one piece and placed on location on a suitable foundation. Whatever your plot requires, we include in the quote.

How long does it take before my home is in place?

That depends on the model, your choices and the permit. Your quote includes a schedule tailored to your situation.

Can I see whether the home fits on my plot?

Yes. In the 3D view, choose “On location”, search for your address and place the home to true scale on your plot, with the existing buildings around it.
