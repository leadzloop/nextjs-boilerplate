Privacy

Privacy statement

How NMK Living handles your data.

NMK Living respects your privacy. This statement explains which data we process when you use our website or request a quote, why we do so and what rights you have. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Who is responsible?

NMK Living (NMK Living) is responsible for processing your personal data. Please send any privacy questions to info@nmkliving.com.

What data do we process?

  • Quote request: name, email address, postcode and, if you enter it, your house number (we complete the address automatically via PDOK), phone number, preferred placement timeframe, purpose and comments.
  • Your configuration: the chosen model and options, so we can prepare a suitable quote.
  • Preferred location (optional): if you place your home on your plot in the 3D view and choose “Use in quote”, we receive the address, the coordinates and the orientation of the home.
  • Technical details: to prevent abuse, we briefly store an encrypted (hashed) version of your IP address. The web server keeps standard log files.

What do we use it for?

Only to prepare and send your quote, to contact you about your request (and for a consultation if you ask for one), and to keep the website secure. The legal basis is taking steps at your request prior to entering into an agreement, and our legitimate interest in security.

How long do we keep it?

We keep data from a quote request for a maximum of 2 years after our last contact, unless it leads to an agreement; in that case the statutory retention periods apply (such as 7 years for accounting records). We keep anti-abuse data for no longer than 24 hours.

Share with others

We never sell your data. It is stored on the servers of our Dutch hosting provider, with whom we have agreements on security. For the address search and aerial photo in the 3D view, we use the public map services of PDOK (Dutch government); your search query is sent to PDOK in the process.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no tracking or advertising cookies and no third-party analytics services. Fonts are loaded from our own server. In your own browser we only store your configuration and preferences (local storage), so you can pick up where you left off; you can clear these yourself.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To do so, send an email to info@nmkliving.com; we will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Security

The website uses an encrypted connection (https). Requests are stored outside the public part of the web server and are accessible only to NMK Living.
